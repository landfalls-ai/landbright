Landbright: identity for people and their agents

An identity provider where every agent has an accountable person, every grant has an end, and Landflow does the work: HR joiners and leavers, approvals in Slack, and granting and revoking access in the systems people actually use.

Pairs with Landflow (JIT engine) Signs in Landflow and Landfall HR sources: HiBob, Shapes Mockups use example data

What we suggest

Six choices that shape everything else, in the order they matter. Each comes from what Landflow and Landfall already do, and from what Apono, Okta and Microsoft Entra have learned about access for agents.

01

Landbright decides and remembers. Landflow acts.

Landbright keeps the directory, the access catalog, the policies and a ledger of every grant. Landflow receives HR events, asks approvers, and provisions and revokes through its plugins.

Apono's model, with connectors and flows as ordinary Landflow plugins and workflows that people can open, read and change.

02

Every agent has a sponsor and no standing secrets

Each Landflow agent gets a twin identity with a sponsor, a risk tier and short-lived tokens. Acting for a person, its token names the person and the agent, scoped to what both may do.

When a sponsor leaves, their agents are suspended until someone else takes them on.

03

Every grant ends, even if Landflow is down

Access is requested for a duration. Landflow revokes at the end, and Landbright revokes its own side (groups, SCIM, tokens) on time whatever happens, then keeps asking until every target confirms.

Permanent access is the exception. It needs an owner, a reason and a review.

04

Start where no code is needed: OIDC and SCIM

Landflow and Landfall already accept OIDC sign-in and SCIM provisioning. Landbright plugs in as their identity provider on day one, and time-bound access to a Landflow project is just a SCIM group.

The first JIT target ships without changing Landflow.

05

Adopt the protocol engine, build the identity core

An OpenID-certified engine (oidc-provider) handles OIDC and OAuth. We build what is ours: agent identities, the grant ledger, policies and the Landflow integration. Keycloak 26.8 is the fallback.

Landfall's first principle, adopt over build, applied to the part that isn't the product.

06

One design language across the family

The same tokens as Landflow (taken from Landfall's design system), Landflow's grouped sidebar, its agent faces, and Landfall's rule that people see ACME REQ-128, never a UUID.

Someone moving from Landflow to Landbright shouldn't notice a seam.

How it fits together

HR events and access requests enter through Landflow and Landbright. Landbright records the decision, and Landflow carries it into each system. Landbright also signs people into Landflow and Landfall directly.

The order of work

Seven waves, each ending with a first slice that works end to end, every suite green and a live check on the local OrbStack stack, the way Landflow ships.

0

Foundations

Nx + NestJS repo, constitution, compose infra (Postgres, Valkey, Mailpit, OpenBao), OpenTelemetry, console shell with the shared tokens.

First sliceOne command brings up a console in Landflow's look with a health page.
1

Identity core and sign-in

People, agents, groups; OIDC provider; passkeys, password + TOTP; Directory and Applications pages.

First sliceA person created in Landbright signs into Landflow with the right role. No Landflow code changed.
2

Provisioning out

SCIM client with retries and reconciliation to Landflow and Landfall; group to project mapping.

First sliceDeactivating someone signs them out of Landflow within seconds.
3

Landflow plugins and HR lifecycle

landbright and hris plugins, the normalized worker record, joiner / mover / leaver templates.

First sliceA signed HiBob employee.joined creates the person, adds birthright groups, and they sign into Landflow on day one.
4

Just-in-time access

Catalog, bundles, policies, requests, the grant ledger and sweeper, the portal, the Approval node (L1).

First slice“Data editor for 2 hours”, asked in the portal, approved in Slack, removed on time, every step on the timeline.
5

Agentic identity

Agent sync, blueprints, sponsors, token exchange with act, ID-JAG, the kill switch, CAEP signals (L2 to L5).

First sliceA Landflow agent asks in Slack for 15 minutes of DB read; the sponsor approves; the call carries person and agent; it ends on time.
6

Policy, reviews, signals

OpenFGA, an AuthZEN endpoint, access reviews an agent drafts and people decide, Landfall break-glass.

First sliceA sev1 in Landfall gives the on-call responder the incident bundle until the room closes, with a note in the room.

Decisions for you

Each has our suggestion. The rest of the plan holds either way.

Name and home

The product's name and where its repo lives.

We suggestlandbright, a private repo under landfalls-ai.

Tenancy

One customer per deployment, or multi-tenant from the start.

We suggestOne per deployment, with the tenant boundary designed in from day one.

Protocol engine

oidc-provider inside NestJS, or Keycloak 26.8 with Landbright as its control plane.

We suggestoidc-provider: the agent model stays in one place and in TypeScript.

Shapes

It has no public API reference, and its webhooks are unsigned.

We suggestAsk Shapes for the GraphQL schema; build HiBob first.

Landflow changes L1 to L5

Approval node, agents:read scope, identity link, agent grant subject, token exchange in runs.

We suggestWrite them as specs in landflow/docs/specs/ now.

First targets after Landflow

GitHub teams, Postgres roles, Google groups, AWS permission sets.

We suggestPostgres and GitHub: Landflow already has their plugins.
Landbright concept · the plan and research are in landbright/docs/plan.md and research.md. All names, people and numbers in the mockups are examples.
